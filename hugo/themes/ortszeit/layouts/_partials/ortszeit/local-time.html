{{- /* The Ortszeit of a page: its date on the wall clock where it was written. The date in front matter already carries the local offset, but the zone in params.time_zone is the truth (offsets change with summer time), so the date is converted explicitly. Without a zone, or with one Hugo does not know, the date stays as written and no clock time is shown. Usage: {{ $t := partial "ortszeit/local-time.html" . }} */ -}} {{- $t := .Date -}} {{- with .Params.time_zone -}} {{- with try (time.In . $t) -}} {{- if not .Err -}}{{- $t = .Value -}}{{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- return $t -}}
